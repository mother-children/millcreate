ABOUT MILL CREATE
Who we are

For over 20 years, MILL CREATE has built garments with fashion brands in Japan and abroad — including brands presenting at Paris Fashion Week. Based in the Bingo region of Hiroshima, we specialize in design proposals, pattern-making, 1st-sample production and OEM/ODM, with a focus on denim. Our name combines Mill (factory) and Create: we don’t just make clothes, we create them. A team of 16 craftspeople works across every stage in-house, so quality is controlled from start to finish.

OUR PEOPLE
Our people are the brand.

Plenty of factories offer in-house production. What sets us apart is the skill and judgment of the people behind each stage. The specialist responsible for each process speaks with you directly — so your intent is understood precisely, and we can propose more than you asked for. The work we receive today comes from the trust placed in our people.

OUR PHILOSOPHY
Creating, not just making

A sample decides whether a garment ever reaches the world. We treat every one of them that way. Behind each is the skill and judgment of the person who made it.

OEM / ODM
From an idea to full production

We cover the entire garment-making process in-house and through our local network: design and specification proposals, pattern-making, grading, spreading and cutting, sewing, special and secondary processing (washing, distressing, printing, embroidery), inspection, and mass production. We work from finished tech packs (OEM) and from early-stage ideas, proposing design, materials and construction ourselves (ODM). We are a manufacturing partner, not just a subcontractor.

OUR STRENGTHS
What we bring
01

Skill and judgment at every stage

Each process is led by a specialist who thinks, decides and proposes. Complex patterns, unusual finishes, specifications with no precedent — we read the intent and offer a better route.

02

Pattern & 1st-sample translation

We read a designer’s intent from sketches and photos — including how a garment should look when worn — and engineer it into precise patterns, correcting to the millimeter. We factor in shrinkage from washing and flag potential mass-production bottlenecks in advance, working with apparel CAD (SUPERα / MYU by YUKA&ALPHA).

03

Strong in heavy denim & difficult builds

From the birthplace of Japanese denim: 14–16oz heavyweight denim, mixed materials, and complex multi-panel constructions are our specialty.

04

Small lots, fast sampling, one-stop

Order from a single unit, with a lead time of roughly 1.5 months. Ahead of a collection we can produce around 100 styles in a month. We cut and sew in-house and coordinate a network within a 10 km radius, for a seamless flow through to mass production.

05

Made in Japan, built on trust

We share our quality standards with our partner factories, so that work done outside our walls is still MILL CREATE work. That is what makes our quality stable and our capacity flexible.
